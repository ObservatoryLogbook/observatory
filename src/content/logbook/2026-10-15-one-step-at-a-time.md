---
title: One step at a time
date: 2026-10-15
---

Sometimes, taking a single step forward can be surprisingly difficult.

For the past couple of years, I have apparently developed a rather peculiar way of walking down stairs. My right leg has a tendency to swing forward instead of simply moving down to the next step. It is completely painless, but it does make ordinary staircases unnecessarily complicated. Wide steps are fine, and so is walking slightly sideways. Apparently, I just need more room to manoeuvre.

Today, instead of doing front squats, my trainer introduced me to a couple of exercises intended to improve my balance, mobility, and control. They look deceptively simple, but they are remarkably challenging. Hopefully, with a little practice, I will eventually learn how to walk down a staircase like a reasonably competent human being.

Other steps are rather more consequential.

Last week, we took an enormous collective step forward at work. Months of preparation culminated in something that went exceptionally well, and it was immensely satisfying to see all that effort pay off.

Next week, another major step awaits us. The preparations are almost complete, and I am genuinely looking forward to it. There is something deeply rewarding about seeing ambitious plans gradually become reality, especially when so many people have contributed to making them happen.

But all these steps come at a price: there are only so many hours in a day, and only so much energy to spend.

At the moment, Observatory has to take a back seat.

Not because I have lost interest. Quite the opposite. There are still so many things I want to build, improve, and explore. But after long working days, early morning training sessions, and everything else that makes up everyday life, there is not always much left in the tank.

I hope to find a couple of hours for Observatory over the weekend. For now, that will have to do.

And perhaps that is perfectly fine. Progress does not have to be continuous to be meaningful.

Sometimes, the next step is simply to wait until you are ready to take it.
