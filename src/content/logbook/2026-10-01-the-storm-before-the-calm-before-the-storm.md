---
title: The storm before the calm before the storm
date: 2026-10-01
---

Today felt like the storm before the calm before the storm.

Several major processes are converging at work at the moment, and today involved the usual mixture of decisions, unexpected problems, rapidly changing priorities and trying to keep everything moving in roughly the right direction. We got there, but it was one of those days where leadership occupied considerably more mental space than the actual work.

It made me think about leadership.

Leadership is strategy, decisions, priorities and turning ideas into reality through other people. One of the things I love about being a leader is seeing people take an idea, make it their own and turn it into something better than I could have produced alone. Good leadership also works upwards: the people around me challenge me, guide me and help me lead better.

But leadership is also profoundly human. It requires attention, presence and an awareness of when somebody needs you to stop managing the work and simply be their manager. Sometimes that means that whatever else seemed important five minutes earlier simply has to wait.

Perhaps that is why the absence of leadership can be felt so immediately as well. Responsibility is considerably easier to carry when you know that somebody is standing behind you.

Today I needed to provide that presence. I could also have used some myself.
