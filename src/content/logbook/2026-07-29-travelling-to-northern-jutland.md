---
title: Traveling to Northern Jutland 
date: 2026-07-29
---

Today was a travel day, taking me from Copenhagen to northern Jutland—about seven hours from door to door. The occasion is a visit with my father-in-law, his girlfriend, and her children, followed by the annual Seafood Festival, which has become something of a tradition.

The journey itself was pleasantly uneventful, exactly as a seven-hour trip involving four different trains ought to be. Even so, spending an entire day travelling is surprisingly draining. Sitting down for hours may not be physically demanding, but it leaves a different kind of tiredness.

One small highlight was finally watching the first episode of season four of *Star Trek: Strange New Worlds*. I will probably always be a *Next Generation* person at heart, but *Strange New Worlds* continues to surprise me. It has managed to capture much of the optimism and sense of exploration that made me fall in love with Star Trek in the first place.

I have to admit that my fingers are itching a little to continue building Observatory. That will have to wait a few more days, though. Development can pause for a while, but fortunately the ideas rarely do.
