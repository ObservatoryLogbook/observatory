No Observatory today. 

Instead, the evening was focused on running with colleagues in the annual DHL relay race, where teams of five runners each run 5K through a maze of tents with barbecues and smoke. It isn't possible to sign up as individuals, instead teams are from companies, and that's why I ran with colleagues. Furthermore, we were in a tent with colleagues from our two sister foundations. So it was another Friday evening spent in the good company of colleagues. However, we didn't support basic free research last night. Not because we didn't have beer, but because only our main competitor's beer was available. Yuck! 

Meanwhile, my partner had a social gathering with his study mates at his school. They each had to bring a dish, so I helped him bake the Banana Dream that we discovered in Northern Jutland almost a month ago. The taste was perfect, and the only thing I would change for next time is fewer bananas. We had added a few more than the recipe called for. This meant that the cake itself wasn't as cohesive as it should have been and that it crumbled easily. However, that is a problem with a solution for next time we try it. Because there will certainly be a next time! 

And as for running: I finished the race in 25:30 which I'm very happy with, considering I really haven't run for three months. Apparently, three months without running is not quite enough to make my legs forget what they are supposed to do. And it helps that my base shape is good, with all the strength training.
