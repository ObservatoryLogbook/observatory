I do most of my thinking in the morning, and today is no different. As mentioned yesterday, today work will finish with the big summer party, and I expect to arrive home very late and have zero energy for the daily Observation. 

Instead I wanted to take a few minutes this morning to jolt down some ideas for Observatory, both for the immediate future and for the longer term. I thought it only fitting after celebrating the first month of Observatory yesterday, that I spend some time looking ahead. 

**Data flow to Training.** Currently I am recording my training on my iPhone in an automatisation, that updates a Numbers file. I then export the relevant table to CSV format, and from there it is moved to my Observatory folders and ingested into the Training page. This needs to be automated, otherwise updates will be patchy at best. 

**Training log visualisation.** I have an idea for a visualisation of when I train: a grid view of every day of the year with the training days coloured in. The heavy training days can be taken directly from CSV file mentioned above, but there needs to be a record of the other training days as well. And again, it needs to be as automatic as possible. 

**Other Engineering pages.** I need an Engineering page to keep track of my recipes and my food thoughts in general. I love to cook, and currently I have some of my best recipes written down by hand in a notebook. Not very convenient for searching or for keeping track of macros or anything like that. Similarly, my partner and I are constantly thinking about vacation ideas, and I believe that would make for a good Engineering page. Finally, I want to make at least one private Engineering page, where I can keep track of my body measurements, i.e., weight, muscle and fat percentages, circumference of shoulder and chest compared to stomach and hips, and blood pressure. 

**Engineering landing page.** Engineering is still under construction, at least until I have a couple more pages and I know what will make sense to record on the landing page. 

**Project pages.** I have a number of small and large projects going on at the moment. I just need to sit down and start working on them, one at a time. The infrastructure is in place. 

**Observatory infrastructure.** There are still a number of small things I want to do on the overall Observatory infrastructure. These include adding a search feature, visitor statistics, and maybe even a custom domain. 

These are all just thoughts and ideas for the moment, and I will need to sit down and flesh them out at some point. They should definitely be able to keep me busy for the coming month and beyond!
