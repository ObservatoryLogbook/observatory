I cannot believe how many hours I spent working on the Observatory today. I am completely exhausted, but also very happy and content. We made significant progress, although much of it happened behind the scenes.

The most visible changes today were:

- **Front page.** The front page no longer presents Projects and Dashboards as separate areas under development. Instead, Projects has become **Science**, reflecting its true purpose as a place for exploration, investigation, and long-running ideas. Dashboards, on the other hand, turned out not to be a destination in their own right. Instead, they belong wherever they provide context, whether inside a science project or as part of the everyday systems that keep life running. That realisation led to an entirely new concept: **Engineering**, which will eventually become home to areas such as training, health, plants, recipes, and other long-lived parts of everyday life. Naturally, I also disappeared down a small rabbit hole searching for the right Lucide icons to represent Science and Engineering.
- **Projects page.** Internally, the page is still called Projects. Renaming the page itself, along with the navigation, can wait until the underlying concepts have settled a little further.
- **Related observations.** Individual project pages now display related observations in reverse chronological order, using exactly the same presentation as the register on the [Logbook page](../logbook). Achieving that required a fair amount of refactoring, but the result is much cleaner code and a reusable component that will benefit the Observatory for a long time.

It was a good day. Every session spent working on the Observatory makes it feel a little more like *my* home, which is ultimately the whole point.

A house rarely feels like home the day you move in. You paint the walls, repair the floors, rearrange the furniture, and gradually make the place your own. Building the Observatory feels much the same. Every small improvement makes it a little more personal, a little more coherent, and a little more like the place I have wanted to create for many years.
