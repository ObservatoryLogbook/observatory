---
title: My trainer lives inside my chiropractor
date: 2026-09-10
---

I am becoming increasingly convinced that my trainer lives inside my chiropractor.

The evidence has been accumulating for a while. A couple of months ago, my chiropractor suggested that when doing heavy top sets of three to five reps, I should first do a single rep at an even heavier weight. Then, when going back down to the weight of the actual top set, it would feel lighter.

My trainer had suggested exactly the same thing the week before.

On Wednesday, I went to see my chiropractor again. This time, he suggested Bulgarian split squats. My trainer has had Bulgarian split squats in my programme for quite some time — and seems to derive particular pleasure from making other people do them.

Coincidence? Perhaps.

Then there are the squat shoes. My trainer recommended them last week. On Wednesday, without knowing this, my chiropractor recommended exactly the same thing.

At some point, Occam's razor has to apply. There is clearly only one reasonable explanation: my trainer lives inside my chiropractor.

Today, I got to test the latest prediction.

I was at the gym early in the morning for the first session with my new squat shoes, and they worked exactly as intended. I could squat deeper, the movement felt much better, and there was almost no strain on my lower back. After all the back issues over the past couple of months, that felt like a major success.

Bench press before that was flying as well. Some of the best reps I have ever done. I am finally starting to feel that I understand the technique rather than merely trying to reproduce it. It still deteriorates a little towards the end of a session, but that increasingly feels like ordinary fatigue rather than a fundamental problem.

So, a very good start to the day.

The rest of it was rather full. A quick trip to work to put out a few remaining fires around the assessment panels, followed by a large conference on the research-funding landscape in Denmark. Good talks, lots of familiar faces and plenty of opportunities to catch up with people. Then back to the Foundation for a two-hour meeting with the facilitators for an upcoming event, followed by a little inbox archaeology before finally heading home.

By then, apparently, my body had had enough.

While helping with dinner, I developed a rather spectacular stomach ache and became increasingly bloated. I have had this happen before, but this was a particularly bad episode. Dinner ended up consisting of a little skyr with ymerdrys, and by 19:50 I was in bed.

No Observatory. No Supabase. No rocket science.

Just a very tired Lars with a very unhappy stomach.

But at least we now have increasingly solid empirical evidence that my trainer and my chiropractor are, in fact, the same person.
