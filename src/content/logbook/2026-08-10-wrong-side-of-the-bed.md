title: The wrong side of the bed
date: 2026-08-10
---

Some days you wake up and immediately know what kind of day it is going to be.

This morning, my alarm woke me at 5:20. That almost never happens. I normally wake up before it, but today I apparently needed every minute of sleep I could get. That set the tone. Training was postponed until tomorrow, work consumed whatever energy was left, and by the time I got home, there wasn't much left in the tank.

It wasn't a bad day, though. Quite the opposite in some ways. Annual blood tests came back looking excellent, dinner was good, my decidedly non-Trekkie partner inexplicably suggested watching Star Trek, and a call with my parents turned into more than an hour of conversation.

Still, it is remarkable how accurately those first few seconds after waking can sometimes predict the rhythm of an entire day.

No Observatory tonight. Some days are for building it. Others are simply for living in it.
