---
title: Beyond time and space
date: 2026-09-02
---

Some friendships go beyond time and space. It’s the kind of friendship where you don’t need to see each other often, but where you’re instantly in tune when you do meet, and where you pick up right where you left off, even if it’s been a couple of months... or more.

My friendship with my Italian friend is like that. She’s wonderful! Tonight we went to our favourite spot, the place we’ve been going to for almost ten years, ever since she first moved to Copenhagen. The conversation was flowing from the first instant, and we enjoyed excellent beers and good food.

Both our partners joined us, and they also get along excellently. As an added source of confusion, they have essentially the same name — differing by just a single letter. Apparently even our choice of partners is somehow in tune.

Unfortunately, of course, time flies far too quickly on an evening when everything just lines up.

And this was on top of the first day after the application deadline, which started in a completely different place: visiting some of the country’s foremost AI researchers to hear what’s going on in the field. I have just one word: fascinating!

All in all, a very good day, going from the more technical to the more social — and I haven’t even covered all the stuff that happened in between.

Tomorrow’s 6:30am training will require a lot of motivation...
