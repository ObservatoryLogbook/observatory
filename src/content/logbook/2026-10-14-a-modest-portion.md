title: A modest portion
date: 2026-10-14
---

Today was a fairly uneventful Wednesday, working from home. I started just before 7 AM, took a long break for training from 11:30 to 13:00, another long break in the afternoon, and finished my last bit of work around 18:30. Not a bad arrangement, actually.

Training was a moderate posterior-chain session: back extensions, Bulgarian split squats with a safety bar, hip thrusts, Nordic curl progressions, and three minutes of skipping. I deliberately held back a little, as my quadratus lumborum had thrown a spectacular tantrum after Sunday’s deadlifts. Interestingly, today’s training caused no problems whatsoever. Perhaps my office chair deserves some credit.

Dinner was another exercise in moderation: fried rice made with 400 g of uncooked rice, 10 eggs, a pointed cabbage, 500 g of carrots, a bunch of spring onions, 500 g of edamame beans, and a little sauce. Just a modest portion, obviously.

By 20:30, I was running out of energy. With my partner due home in about 45 minutes, I considered doing some Observatory work, but decided against it. Yesterday’s Training History was finished, tested, and running beautifully, with zero errors, zero warnings, and zero hints.

Sometimes the most sensible engineering decision is to leave a perfectly functioning system alone.

So tonight, the Observatory can take care of itself. I’m heading for the sofa.
