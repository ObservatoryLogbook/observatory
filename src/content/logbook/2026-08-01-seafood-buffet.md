Today’s observation is, as Mr. Spock would put it, “fascinating.”

My in-laws sold one of their puppies, and today its new family came to collect it. Over the past three days my partner had become remarkably attached to the little dog, and when it left, he reacted emotionally. I never grew up around animals and never had a pet, so those emotions do not come naturally to me. He, on the other hand, is different. Watching that difference between us was oddly fascinating, and one of the many reasons I love him.

The highlight of the trip was, without question, the enormous seafood buffet at the harbour.

![Seafood buffet](images/2026-08-01-seafood-buffet.jpeg)

I could happily live on seafood. Everything here is incredibly fresh: langoustines, crabs, shrimp, and countless other kinds of seafood, much of which you shell yourself at the table - call it a working dinner in the best possible sense. There were also fish cakes, fish soup, fried fish, and several other prepared dishes. Everything was absolutely delicious. I may, purely in the interest of scientific completeness, have eaten slightly more than strictly necessary.

Sharing the evening with friends and family somehow made the food taste even better. Good seafood deserves good company.
