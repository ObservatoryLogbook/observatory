---
title: The end of the summer
date: 2026-07-31
---

It is the last day of July, and it already feels as though the weather has started drifting towards autumn. I am sure there will still be warm summer days ahead, but those are often the nicest because they arrive as an unexpected gift.

Rather than spending the day sightseeing, we visited different branches of the family instead. Some I had met before, others were new to me, but everywhere I felt genuinely welcome.

In the evening we put together a small collection of treats: cheeses and cured sausages, followed by a selection of sugar-free sweets, and dried peaches coated in chocolate. The peaches were so good that they deserve a special mention.

The quiet pace suited me well. I am definitely beginning to feel the accumulated lack of sleep, and today was perhaps the first day where I noticed it more than I tried to ignore it.
