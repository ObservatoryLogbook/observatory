The Training page is beginning to take shape. We now have a nice layout and language for Current goals (including what has already been achieved), Current focus, and Training context.

The difference between the three is:

- **Current goals.** These are my overall training goals, divided into Strength and Skills. Strength goals specify weights and repetitions for my main lifts: squat, bench press, and deadlift. Skills are specific things I am working towards being able to do, such as unassisted dips, unassisted pull-ups, and the current one: a handstand. Once a goal has been achieved, it moves into the Achieved section with the appropriate date, and I can define a new goal. For Strength, only the most recently achieved goal for each lift is displayed.
- **Current focus.** In order to achieve the above goals, I have certain focuses in my training. For example, I am currently doing pike push-ups as part of the progression towards a handstand, and I have a specific subgoal for the bench press: 90 kg. This section also gives me room to describe the current focus of my different training sessions and programmes.
- **Training context.** Life happens, even for me. Just over a month ago, I had a small lower-back issue, which has primarily affected my deadlifts. Earlier in the year, I had a bruised rib after a collision with another bike, which affected my bench press. I want to keep some record of these issues because they provide explanations and footnotes to my overall progress.

A few additional definitions are useful for Training context. I record these as structured data with four possible statuses:

- **Acute.** The problem is currently affecting my training.
- **Recovering.** The immediate problem is improving, and I am moving back towards normal training capacity.
- **Residual.** The acute problem has passed, but there are still lingering effects on my training or performance.
- **Resolved.** The issue no longer has a meaningful effect on my training.

All of this information is now displayed on the Training page in Engineering. Not bad for a Sunday's work.

The day was broken up by a training session, where my trainer coincidentally asked about my current goals, and I could show him the page under development. Talk about good timing!
