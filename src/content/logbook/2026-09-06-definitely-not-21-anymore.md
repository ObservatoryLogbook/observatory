---
title: Definitely not 21 anymore
date: 2026-09-06
---

It is unbelievable how time flies when you’re having fun. Here I mean both the last two days on the island and the past almost 30 years of coming here. It seems like only yesterday that we first arrived on the island. We had just turned 21. We were young and full of energy, inexperienced in life but full of confidence.

As always, Sunday was a bit slow, and we were a bit low on energy. On Sunday, we were definitely not 21 anymore.

We had some leftover fish, which made for a very nice and substantial brunch, consisting of warm-smoked halibut, fried bakskuld, bread and other cold cuts. The weather was finally nice enough that we could sit outside and enjoy this last meal on the island.

We cleaned the summer house in about an hour and drove to the ferry — only to be met by the longest queue ever! The expected waiting time was four hours. FOUR HOURS! The reason was, once again, the festival with its 11,000 guests. Not all of them were travelling by car, sure, but the ferries were still completely overloaded.

In the end, we “only” waited 2.5 hours before we could say goodbye to the island. Thankfully, we had left a bit early, so after a long drive home, I arrived around 19:30.

My partner had made dinner, and I was asleep well before 21:00… the sleep was needed!
