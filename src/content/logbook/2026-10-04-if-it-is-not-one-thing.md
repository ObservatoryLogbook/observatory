Sunday began exactly as a Sunday should.

I went through my morning measurements and, in the process, gave the Observatory input pages a fairly thorough workout. Everything simply worked. There is something deeply satisfying about using a system that has taken months to build and discovering that it has quietly become infrastructure.

Then training with my trainer, where we added another kind of infrastructure.

I have been saying for a while that I would like to lose some fat mass and get my weight down to at least 95 kg, perhaps a little lower. The problem is rather obvious: I do not consistently track what I eat. That makes it surprisingly difficult to manage either energy intake or macros with any intention.

So today we shook hands on it. I track my food, and at the end of each day I send him three emojis:

🍽️ 👍 💪

That is all. No spreadsheet, no report and no elaborate accountability system. Just confirmation that I did the thing.

The commitment is no longer only to training. It is also to the food that supports it.

Later, my partner and I began the conversation we should have before deciding whether to live together. We already knew that we agreed on most of the big things, but agreement is not quite the same as having said things out loud.

There were discoveries.

Apparently, my partner describes his interior style as *whimsical*. This includes room for interesting stones found outside and flowers picked by the side of the road.

I am processing this information.

We also called my grandmother to wish her a happy 96th birthday. She is now a decidedly mature lady and, rather wonderfully, still perfectly capable of talking to us through an iPad on FaceTime.

And then the emails arrived.

Something fairly critical had gone wrong at work, at exactly the wrong moment. What followed was a small Sunday-afternoon emergency operation: messages, temporary workarounds, people contacted and troubleshooting initiated.

It is still not fixed. At this point, however, it is also no longer mine to fix. Someone else has the problem now and has promised to let me know when it is resolved.

There is a phrase we use at work:

*If it isn't one thing, it's fucking another.*

Dinner is made. Star Trek is available. The right people have my phone number.

That will have to do for tonight.
