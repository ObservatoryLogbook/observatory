This morning started strong. Quite literally.

I benched 90 kg.

It has been one of my intermediate goals for a while, on the way towards the considerably more intimidating 100 kg, and this morning it finally happened. My trainer and I even recorded it, so there is now video evidence that the bar did, in fact, go back up.

And it felt fantastic.

The rest of the session was good too. Squats felt strong, and we experimented with side extensions as another way of strengthening my lower back. They will require a little more practice, but after the recent irritation in my quadratus lumborum, getting stronger rather than merely being careful seems like a worthwhile project.

Then I went to work.

By 10 o'clock I had already dealt with a technical problem that temporarily made saved draft assessments disappear for our panel members, an unreachable reviewer who had completed precisely zero assessments by the deadline, another problem related to the upcoming centre negotiations, and the beginning of what became a very substantial quantity of email. The rest of the day continued in much the same spirit.

But by the time I left around six, things were remarkably close to being under control. There are still notes to polish and details to check over the weekend, but we are probably about as ready for next week's four panel meetings as we can reasonably be.

And for once, that is not what I want to remember about today.

I want to remember lying on the bench at 6.30 in the morning with 90 kg above me, pushing the bar upwards and realizing that I could actually do it.

90 down. 100 to go.
