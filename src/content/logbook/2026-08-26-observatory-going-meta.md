I was so tired yesterday that I didn't even notice the most obvious thing for yesterday's observation: how the observations helped me keep track of old design and naming choices, meaning that the Logbook was actually working like a logbook! Now that is meta. 

Luckily I had a good night's sleep last night, and I realised this obvious thing. 

I was even so well rested this morning, that I didn't mind my trainer putting me through the hoops for nearly 1.5 hours, which came on top of the more than 20 minutes of warm-up. I definitely think I will have a good night's sleep again tonight. 

Not everything was about sleep today. It was a nice and quiet day at work with only a single minor meeting. Which meant, I could be productive on my own, and I enjoyed it thoroughly. 

It didn't stop me from also being productive on Observatory. I have been meaning to write a few words about the books I want to read, as well as a few words about the books I have read. There is nothing like creating the actual setup for that to motivate me to actually now start doing it. This exercise went surprisingly smooth, again because everything is beginning to be set up to handle tasks like this. 

The rest of the evening was spent aligning design choices across the pages. For example the multiple colours of the cards on Observatory, Science and Engineering, the slightly different font sizes, the slightly different line colours and widths, and so on and so forth. The colours should not be slightly different shades of the same colour: either they are the same, or they are markedly different, not something and nothing at the same time. 

Making all of these changes did eventually wear me out so much that I ended up making silly mistakes. Before starting a full debugging session on why nothing changes, or doesn't change in the way I expect, it helps to save the file that is supposed to make changes appear... yes, that was a mistake on my part. 

Turns out, I am becoming tired again after all!
