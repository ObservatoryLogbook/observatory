I really should not have worked on Observatory tonight.

I woke up at 1 AM last night and only managed to get another couple of hours of sleep around 4. After a full day at work, I was very much ready for bed. There was only one problem: my partner was not home yet, and since his key currently does not work, I had to stay awake.

Apparently, the solution was Observatory.

On Sunday, my trainer mentioned FFMI, Fat-Free Mass Index, as a potentially useful metric for Sick Bay. So tonight I went down that particular rabbit hole. It turns out that there is quite a bit to consider, especially when you are 196 cm tall and start looking into different approaches to height adjustment.

In the end, I decided to keep things simple. Observatory now calculates standard FFMI from my daily Karada measurements, using fat-free mass divided by height squared. Looking back at 397 measurements since July 2025 was particularly satisfying: despite considerable changes in weight and body fat, my monthly average FFMI has remained remarkably stable.

Current status now shows the 7-day average, currently 20.5 kg/m².

Not bad for an evening where the original plan was to do absolutely nothing.

And now my partner really should be home soon.
