---
title: Why so tired?
date: 2026-08-24
---

I often find myself tired for no apparent reason at all. That is, until I start thinking about what I actually did.

Take today, for example. I woke up before 5am and lay in bed for a little while before getting up and getting ready to go to the gym at 6am. I did a strenuous warm-up session of almost 30 minutes, consisting of various stretches and dynamic warm-up exercises. Then I did three sets of deadlifts before giving up because my lower back was killing me, followed by a near-PR set of bench presses.

I then went home, showered, had a protein shake, did some dishes from last night, filled the dishwasher, did a little light cleaning, and biked to work. There, I had a full day of mostly answering questions, either from colleagues or from potential applicants, interrupted by a couple of long meetings. I don't know how many emails I sent, but it was more than 30.

I then biked home and did the grocery shopping on the way. At home, I cooked dinner: kebab salad with a homemade skyr dressing and lots of chopped vegetables, primarily summer cabbage.

Now I'm in front of the laptop. I've just finished implementing a mobile menu on Observatory because, with Engineering up and running, the screen real estate had become too crowded on a small screen.

So why am I so tired? Part of the reason is certainly physical: I did a lot of hard work. The other part is more psychological. Apart from all the work stuff, my partner needs to bring a cake to work on Friday. He would like it to be rainbow-themed. And he would like it to be this flavour, for that many people, and... guess who's baking it?

And perhaps worst of all... he forgot to buy toilet paper.

He's sweet and adoring and lovable, so of course I'll forgive him. Maybe I will even one day tell him that I knew this was going to happen ten days ago, when I noticed he had taken the last roll but didn't let me know...
