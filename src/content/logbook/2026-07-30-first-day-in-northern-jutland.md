title: First day in Northern Jutland 
date: 2026-07-30
---

Sorry for sharing intimate details, but today’s observation is remarkably simple: a queen-size bed is simply too small for two grown men. Case closed.

As a result, my energy levels have been rather limited today—and, fortunately, today turned out to be the perfect day for exactly that.

The day has been completely uneventful. We spent the morning relaxing with the family before heading to the local gym with my father-in-law’s stepson for a light workout. In the afternoon we went for a short drive, enjoyed some excellent ice cream, and then returned for another quiet evening together.

Normally I find it difficult to justify an unproductive day, but today never asked anything of me. It was simply a day to slow down, recover from a poor night’s sleep, and enjoy good company.

Recovery from today’s lack of sleep may well have to wait until we return to Copenhagen. There are still four nights left in the queen-size bed.
