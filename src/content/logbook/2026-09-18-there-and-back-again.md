title: There and back again
date: 2026-09-18

No Observatory today.

It is amazing how a long day of doing almost nothing can feel completely exhausting. Until you realise that maybe, just maybe, you didn't do nothing at all. Instead, the day was full of lots of little things.

Today was such a day.

I woke up early to take the train to Aarhus, a journey of almost two and a half hours. Luckily, because it was work-related, we can travel first class, which offers a bit more leg room, free coffee and water, light refreshments, and lots of quiet to work in.

So I sat in front of my computer and quietly worked for the entire train ride. Then I took a taxi up to visit another Pioneer Centre, where we talked to the centre leadership, the early-career researchers, each other, and so on, before walking back down to catch the train home.

On the way back, the train ride took almost an hour longer than expected. But I just worked away.

And now I'm home. Twelve hours after I left.

And I'm dead.

At least I got some fantastic news on the train, which once again goes to show just how much I love my work and my workplace.

But Observatory is officially closed until tomorrow, when I will have a lot more time on my hands.
