---
title: A weekend of contrasts
date: 2026-10-11
---

The weekend turned out to be a study in contrasts.

After an exceptionally demanding week at work, Saturday was almost entirely devoted to recovery. There was little energy for anything beyond resting, and several naps punctuated a day in which very little was accomplished. It was, quite simply, a day on standby.

Sunday, however, was a different story. Energy returned, and with it came a remarkably productive day. Training was followed by a substantial round of household chores: laundry, fresh bed linen, bathroom cleaning, vacuuming, grocery shopping, and an extensive session of meal preparation for the coming week. A considerable amount of domestic maintenance was finally brought up to date.

There was also time for significant progress on Observatory. A new integration between Apple Shortcuts and Supabase was completed, allowing the Gym Finish shortcut to register training days automatically through a dedicated Edge Function. The implementation involved authentication, database permissions, date validation, and a few rounds of debugging before the first successful registration arrived directly from the iPhone.

By the end of Sunday, both the apartment and Observatory were in considerably better shape than they had been 24 hours earlier.

The contrast between the two days was striking, but perhaps not entirely coincidental. Saturday’s inactivity may have been exactly what made Sunday’s productivity possible. Rest, it seems, is sometimes an essential part of getting things done.
