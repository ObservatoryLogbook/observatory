The theme of the day is undoubtedly invisible work.

During the day, I have made a number of decisions, big and small. I have been in touch with a lot of people, coordinated efforts across Communication, interacted with Economy, held status meetings and tracked updates in my own unit, talked to external collaborators, even a Board member for a brief second, and emailed with about 20 panel members.

Still, I feel like I did nothing today. I haven't produced any real, concrete results of my own, so to speak, but I have been at the centre of a number of efforts. It is something I am still working on coming to terms with: these days, by far most of my results happen by proxy. This is not so different from when I was a PhD and postdoc supervisor, where the young people would do most of the work, but I always kept a project or two for myself so I could get that feeling of personal accomplishment.

Tonight's work on Observatory was not too different conceptually. I have made a large number of changes that will forever be invisible. I have standardised colours across Observatory, and I have made `global.css` the source of truth by defining colour variables that are now used throughout. This meant making microscopic adjustments to a few colours here and there, but nothing that is visible to my eye.

I'll keep repeating to myself that my day was productive. And I know that by any external measure, it was a highly productive day. That doesn't mean it was a fulfilling day, though, but then again, not every day has to be. And that is OK.
