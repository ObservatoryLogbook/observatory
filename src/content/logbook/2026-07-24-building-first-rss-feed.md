date: 2026-07-24

Eventually, this little corner of the website will hopefully be discovered by more people than just me and my significant other. At that point, an RSS feed will be useful, allowing readers to follow new updates to the Logbook.

For this reason, today was all about implementing an RSS feed. Thankfully, Astro makes that relatively easy and straightforward. I tackled the exercise primarily by reading the Astro documentation and building a first version myself, although the implementation still needed a few adjustments. I have a trusted partner in crime while building this website, and they helped me bring it across the finishing line. It felt particularly good to begin with the documentation myself and then rely on guidance to resolve the remaining questions.

The current version of the RSS feed is still rather basic because the surrounding website architecture is being built at the same time. Eventually, each update will point to its own logbook entry instead of merely linking to the complete Logbook. For now, however, the focus needs to shift towards implementing a mobile workflow, so that will be the priority over the next few days.

Stay tuned for updates!
