title: An all-day meeting
date: 2026-08-20
---

Some days are unique. Today was such a day.

One of the primary goals of the organisation I work for is to support independent basic research. Today we did so to the tune of DKK 150 million, or approximately EUR 20 million. Following a process that started more than two years ago, today was the culmination: the final decisions were made about whom to support and with how much.

It is a unique feeling to sit in the room when such decisions are made. My role is not to make the decisions, but to do everything I can to support the people who do. Today, that role expanded to supporting the applicants who came in for interviews with the decision-makers. There is a very special atmosphere on a day like this.

For that reason, I am completely exhausted, and there will be no Observatory today apart from this observation. Tomorrow is the annual summer party at work, so I don't expect any Observatory then either. I'll be back on Saturday!

And then I realised something: today marks exactly one month since Observatory went online. Somehow, during that month, it has grown from a small website into something much larger, with projects, dashboards, design systems, icons, colours, training data, and countless decisions about tiny details.

Most importantly, there has been an observation every single day. Including today. Apparently, even an all-day meeting and complete exhaustion couldn’t break the streak.

Happy one-month anniversary, Observatory!
