---
title: Little surprises
date: 2026-08-02
---

Today’s walk began with an encounter I could happily have skipped: clouds of flies that seemed determined to follow us wherever we went. Fortunately, the walk also offered something rather more interesting. Hidden among the trees was a small holy spring, one of those quiet little places you would never know existed unless someone showed you the way.

The rest of the day unfolded at a similarly gentle pace. We went shopping with my father-in-law, I managed to catch up on a little rest, and later we visited relatives. Family histories can be surprisingly complicated: my partners mothers side of the family tree certainly is.

The culinary highlight, however, came with the afternoon coffee. Someone had baked a banana cake topped with the coconut topping from a Danish *drømmekage*. It was such an obvious combination that I immediately wondered why I had never encountered it before. Some ideas seem so natural that they almost feel like discoveries rather than inventions. I made a note to recreate it once I get home.

The day ended much as the rest of the holiday has: one final ice cream, leftovers from yesterday’s seafood feast, and a quiet evening before bed.

If there is one observation worth recording today, it is this: sometimes the best discoveries are remarkably small. A forgotten spring in the woods. An unexpected family story. Or simply the realisation that banana cake and *drømmekage* belong together.
