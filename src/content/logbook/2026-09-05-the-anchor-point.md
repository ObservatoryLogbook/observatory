title: The anchor point
date: 2026-09-05
---

Did I mention I’m an A-type person? Even though we went to bed at 1:30am, I still managed to wake up at 6:30… which was even late by my standards! Thankfully, my friends let me have a rest in the middle of the day.

The day was surprisingly well organised. Maybe because we had booked a mobile sauna on the beach from 15:00 to 17:00, and we needed to be there for that — it was the anchor point of the day. Normally, we bike to one of the two main towns on the island for lunch and to buy groceries for dinner, but (a) we didn’t have bikes, and (b) we didn’t have time. It’s difficult to make everything happen when the others don’t get up until 10, and we first have to have breakfast.

We planned ahead yesterday and made sure that we had enough for breakfast, lunch *and* dinner. It was very unusual for us to plan that far ahead, but the mobile sauna forced us to.

The mobile sauna was definitely the highlight of the day: running into the 17–18°C water, splashing around for 5–10 minutes, then running back to the sauna and having a beer inside before running back out was fantastic — and very invigorating! We had never tried it before, but I have a feeling it won’t be the last time.

Back at the summer house, we had a couple of gin and tonics and talked and talked. Somehow, this turned into an elaborate discussion about the octopus from yesterday. Seven of its arms were noticeably smaller than the eighth, which naturally led us to speculate that they had all previously been eaten by sharks and grown back. This, in turn, led to the important statistical question of what the probability was that one particular friend had ended up with the octopus’s only original, never-eaten, “virgin” arm. Thankfully, we had ChatGPT available to estimate it. These are the important questions in life.

Eventually, we cooked dinner: hake and monkfish fillets with grilled vegetables and roasted potatoes. Delicious!

Everybody was a little tired, so we went to bed around 1am… without the midnight hot dogs! Definitely a mistake…
