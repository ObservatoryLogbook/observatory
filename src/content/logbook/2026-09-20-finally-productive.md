Over the past few weeks, I have struggled with feeling productive: that sense of progression, where you feel that things start coming together, piece by piece, and it is your doing. And where you can actually see and feel progress as it happens. And I know that when I look at what I do during the day, I am far from unproductive, but still, I haven't had that *feeling* of being productive.

I finally had it today.

Sick Bay version 1.0 is now ready. I am keeping it private, as it contains personal data, but the first complete version is now done. This morning I wrapped up the segmental body analysis with a segmental fat analysis. One thing is coding the component so it actually works: that is important, but went along fairly smoothly. However, seeing the results, and seeing how my body fat is almost walking away, was very satisfying. I guess all the hard work is paying off. Who would have known!

Speaking of, I did have a session with my trainer this morning, and I showed him Sick Bay. He was duly impressed! He also suggested calculating my FFMI: Fat Free Mass Index. I'll have to dive deep into that in the not too distant future.

Back home, it was time to integrate my training data into Supabase. We created the database and a new input page for training data, following the design of the Sick Bay input page almost to the letter. We of course tested it thoroughly before importing all my historical training data and finally letting the actual Training page ingest data directly from Supabase. It was so satisfying to see.

Especially because before, I would finish a training session and record my top set values in a Numbers sheet. This would then occasionally be converted to CSV before being ingested into Observatory. Now I can record the data directly in Observatory after training, and the Training page gets its data directly from Supabase. It is almost automatic, which is incredibly satisfying.

So much progress on Observatory today, which is why the day felt so incredibly productive.

Besides that, my partner and I are celebrating our two-year anniversary tomorrow, but we started the celebrations a bit early today...
