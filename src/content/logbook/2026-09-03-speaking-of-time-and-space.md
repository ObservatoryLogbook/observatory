From tomorrow until Sunday I will be offline. I will still write my daily observations, but only on my phone, and then I will commit them and update Observatory on Sunday evening.

The reason follows naturally from yesterday's theme of friendships transcending time and space. I will be going to the small island of Fanø, where I have been going since 1999 with three of my best friends from high school. We have gone there almost every single year since then, and it is one of the highlights of the year. A long weekend where we're basically back in high school, despite what life has thrown at us over the past 25+ years.

We have specific traditions, things we always do, and we always leave room for new experiences. Last year, one of my friends had borrowed four Puch Maxi mopeds, and we went all over the island on the little machines, with men our age staring longingly at us as we passed. Truly legendary, and something that can only happen on Fanø.

Today also followed nicely from yesterday's observation, especially the ending. I found the motivation, and I trained. Not just that, I trained with my trainer, and he had been looking at Observatory. We are currently planning how to progress from my current bench press of 82.5 kg to 100 kg, and he was thinking about it in Observatory terms. I love it!

Besides, I am finally back to squatting somewhat normally after my lower-back issues. It feels good!

The rest of the day went by in a flurry of work. But now, I am off to pack the last things before the annual island trip!
