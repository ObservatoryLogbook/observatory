The second week of August is Pride Week in Copenhagen, of course culminating in the great Pride parade through parts of the city.

My partner and I joined, but only after stopping for a luxury ice cream. Summer is still going strong, and we only ate it for health reasons: obviously, it is important to stay cool!

The Pride parade itself was an incredible celebration of diversity in all its shapes and forms. There was also very loud music, and towards the end I was completely overstimulated. We had a very early dinner at a buffet restaurant before heading home again.

It was a very good day. Now I need to relax.
