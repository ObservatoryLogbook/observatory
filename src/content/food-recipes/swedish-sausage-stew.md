## Method

### Preparation

1. Peel the potatoes and cut into bite-sized cubes, approximately 2 cm.
2. Peel the carrots and cut into small cubes, approximately 1 cm.
3. Divide the cauliflower into small florets, roughly the size of the carrot pieces.
4. Finely chop the onions and dice the red pepper.
5. Slice the sausages into thick rounds.

### Cooking the potatoes and vegetables

1. Bring a large saucepan of lightly salted water to the boil.
2. Add the potatoes and carrots.
3. Cook for approximately 7–8 minutes.
4. Add the cauliflower and continue cooking for another 4–5 minutes.
5. Drain when the vegetables are nearly tender but still hold their shape.
6. Set aside while preparing the sauce.

### Making the paprika sauce

1. Melt the butter in a large, heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onions and cook for approximately 5–7 minutes until softened.
3. Add the diced red pepper and cook for another 3–4 minutes.
4. Add the sliced sausages and fry briefly to develop some colour.
5. Stir in the tomato purée and sweet paprika.
6. Cook for approximately 1 minute, stirring continuously, to develop the flavour without burning the paprika.
7. Add the stock and cooking cream.
8. Stir thoroughly and bring to a gentle simmer.
9. Simmer for approximately 5 minutes.

### Combining and finishing

1. Add the cooked potatoes, carrots and cauliflower to the sauce.
2. Fold everything together gently to avoid breaking up the potatoes.
3. Simmer over low heat for another 5–10 minutes, until the vegetables are fully tender and the sauce has thickened slightly.
4. Add a little more stock or water if the sauce becomes too thick.
5. Season with salt and black pepper.
6. Serve hot.

## Notes

- The vegetables should be cut relatively small so they become an integrated part of the dish rather than dominating it.
- The potatoes remain the main vegetable component, preserving the character of traditional Swedish sausage stew.
- The carrots contribute gentle sweetness, while the cauliflower adds volume without introducing a particularly strong flavour.
- The red pepper should be finely diced to blend into the paprika and tomato sauce.
- Use sweet paprika rather than smoked paprika.
- The sauce should be creamy and distinctly flavoured with tomato and paprika.
- The dish can be prepared ahead of time and reheated gently. Add a little water or milk if the sauce thickens during storage.
