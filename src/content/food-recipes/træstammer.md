## Method

### Make the filling

Blend the black beans, dates, desiccated coconut, oat flour, cocoa powder, vanilla paste, salt, raspberry jam, rum essence and coffee.

Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

Shape into logs.

### Assemble

Roll out the marzipan into a long band.

Wrap the marzipan around the filling and cut into 10 pieces.

### Finish

Dip the ends in melted dark chocolate.

Decorate with sprinkles.
