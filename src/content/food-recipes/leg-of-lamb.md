## Method

Mix all the ingredients for the rub. Coat the leg of lamb thoroughly with the mixture, wrap tightly in cling film and refrigerate for 24 hours.

Place the lamb in a roasting dish with the rosemary, garlic and red wine. Roast at 110°C for 12 hours.

For the sauce, sauté the chilli, red onion and garlic.

Add the fresh tomatoes and tomato purée and fry briefly.

Add the chopped tomatoes, espresso, brown sugar and Tabasco. Season with salt and pepper and simmer for 45 minutes.

Add the red pepper and cook for another 5 minutes.

Blend the sauce until smooth.

Serve with roasted flatbread and tzatziki.
