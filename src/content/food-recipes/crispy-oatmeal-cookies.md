## Method

### Prepare the dough

Melt the butter.

Whisk the eggs and sugar until light and fluffy.

Mix all ingredients together.

### Shape

Place spoonfuls of dough on a baking sheet using a teaspoon. They spread out when baked. 

### Bake

Bake at 180°C for 8 minutes.

The cookies can be dipped in chocolate or made into sandwich cookies with buttercream, etc.
