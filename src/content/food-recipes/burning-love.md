## Method

### Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan.
2. Peel the beetroot and cut into approximately 2 cm cubes.
3. Peel and thinly slice the onions.
4. Peel the potatoes and cut into evenly sized chunks.
5. Cut the cauliflower into florets.

### Roasting the beetroot

1. Toss the beetroot with 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, black pepper and the optional thyme.
2. Spread evenly across a large baking tray, preferably in a single layer.
3. Roast for approximately 35–45 minutes, turning once, until tender and lightly caramelised.
4. Remove from the oven and toss with 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar.
5. Taste and add up to another tablespoon if more acidity is needed.

### Caramelising the onions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-low heat.
2. Add the sliced onions and a small pinch of salt.
3. Cook slowly for approximately 25–35 minutes, stirring regularly.
4. If the onions begin to stick or brown too quickly, add a splash of water and reduce the heat.
5. Continue until the onions are very soft, golden and naturally sweet.
6. Keep warm until serving.

### Making the mash

1. Place the potatoes in a large saucepan and cover with cold water.
2. Bring to the boil and cook for approximately 15 minutes.
3. Add the cauliflower florets and continue cooking for another 10–12 minutes, until both vegetables are completely tender.
4. Drain thoroughly and return to the warm saucepan.
5. Allow the vegetables to steam dry for 2–3 minutes to remove excess moisture.
6. Mash thoroughly using a potato masher.
7. Add the butter and Philadelphia cream cheese, stirring until evenly incorporated.
8. Gradually add 100–150 ml warm milk until the desired consistency is reached.
9. Season generously with salt, black pepper and a small pinch of nutmeg.

### Preparing the bacon

1. Place the diced bacon in a frying pan over medium heat.
2. Fry until golden and crisp, stirring occasionally.
3. Transfer to kitchen paper to drain excess fat.
4. Keep the bacon separate from the other components until serving.

### Serving

1. Spoon a generous portion of potato and cauliflower mash onto each plate.
2. Top with a substantial amount of caramelised onions.
3. Add the roasted beetroot alongside.
4. Serve the crisp bacon separately so that it can be added according to preference.

## Notes

- This is a vegetable-rich interpretation of the traditional Danish dish *brændende kærlighed*.
- The mash combines potatoes and cauliflower, with cream cheese providing additional richness.
- Drain the cauliflower thoroughly to avoid a watery mash.
- Use a potato masher rather than a blender or food processor, which can make the potatoes gluey.
- The onions should be cooked slowly to develop sweetness without burning.
- Roasted beetroot replaces the pickled beetroot traditionally served with the dish.
- Balsamic vinegar is important for balancing the sweetness of the onions and beetroot.
- Bacon is served separately rather than mixed into the onions.
- The components can be prepared in advance and reheated separately. Reheat the mash gently with a little extra milk if necessary.
