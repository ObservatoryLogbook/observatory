## Method

### Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan.
2. Line two large baking trays with baking parchment.
3. Wash the potatoes and cut into approximately 2 cm pieces.
4. Cut the cabbage into broad strips or wedges, keeping some of the leaves together to prevent them from drying out.
5. Peel the red onions and cut into wedges.
6. Core the apple and cut into thick wedges.
7. Keep the raw sausages refrigerated until needed.

### Roasting the potatoes

1. Toss the potatoes with 2 tbsp olive oil, salt and black pepper.
2. Spread across the first baking tray in a single layer.
3. Roast for approximately 20 minutes before adding the second tray.
4. Turn the potatoes to encourage even browning.

### Roasting the sausages and vegetables

1. Toss the cabbage, red onions and apple with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt and black pepper.
2. Spread the vegetables across the second baking tray.
3. Arrange the raw sausages on top, leaving some space between them.
4. Place the tray in the oven alongside the potatoes.
5. Roast for approximately 25–35 minutes, depending on the thickness of the sausages.
6. Turn the sausages halfway through cooking and gently rearrange the vegetables if necessary.
7. Check that the sausages are fully cooked, with an internal temperature of at least 75°C in the centre.
8. If the vegetables are browning too quickly before the sausages are cooked, loosely cover the vegetables with foil or move them to a separate dish.
9. Check that the potatoes are tender and golden. Continue roasting for a few additional minutes if necessary.

### Making the skyr dressing

1. Combine the skyr and crushed garlic in a bowl.
2. Add 1 tbsp lemon juice and stir thoroughly.
3. Gradually add water until the dressing has a creamy, spoonable consistency.
4. Season with salt and black pepper.
5. Taste and add more lemon juice if needed.
6. Refrigerate until serving.

### Serving

1. Divide the roasted potatoes, cabbage, red onions and apple between plates.
2. Add the cooked sausages.
3. Serve the garlic and lemon skyr dressing separately.

## Notes

- This recipe uses raw apple and thyme sausages, which must be cooked thoroughly before serving.
- The sausages already contain apple and thyme, so no additional thyme is included in the initial version.
- Using only one apple prevents the dish from becoming excessively sweet.
- Roast the potatoes separately from the cabbage to prevent moisture from softening their surfaces.
- Use two large baking trays to avoid overcrowding. If the vegetables are piled too deeply, they will steam rather than roast.
- Keep the cabbage pieces relatively large to prevent them from drying out or burning.
- Pointed cabbage cooks more quickly than white cabbage. Check the vegetables during roasting and remove them early if necessary.
- The cooking time of the sausages depends on their thickness. Check the internal temperature rather than relying on time alone.
- The skyr dressing provides a cool, acidic contrast to the roasted vegetables and sausages.
- Refrigerate leftovers promptly and reheat the sausages thoroughly before serving.
- Store the skyr dressing separately and serve cold.
