## Method

Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm water.

Blend the cottage cheese and eggs with an immersion blender and stir into the dissolved yeast.

Add the salt and flour and knead in a stand mixer.

Leave the dough to rise for 60 minutes.

Divide and shape into two loaves or rolls.

Leave to proof for another 30 minutes.

Bake at 200°C. Bake loaves for approximately 40 minutes, covering them after 20 minutes. Bake rolls for approximately 15 minutes.

Grains and seeds can be added to taste.
