## Method

### Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Wash the potatoes thoroughly and cut them in half lengthways.
3. Place the potato halves on a baking tray and brush with oil.
4. Drain and rinse the black beans.
5. Ensure the prepared meat sauce is ready to reheat.

### Cooking

1. Bake the potato halves until tender and cooked through. The cooking time will depend on their size.
2. Meanwhile, heat the meat sauce thoroughly in a large saucepan.
3. Stir the black beans into the meat sauce and heat through.
4. Remove the potatoes from the oven once tender.
5. Spoon the meat sauce and bean mixture generously over the potato halves.
6. Distribute the grated cheese over the filling.
7. Add pickled jalapeños to taste.
8. Return the loaded potatoes to the oven until the cheese has melted and the filling is piping hot.
9. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- The filling uses approximately 1.5–1.6 kg of prepared [Big-Batch Beef Bolognese](/engineering/food/big-batch-beef-bolognese/) before adding the black beans.
- Adding 500 g black beans increases the filling to approximately 2 kg.
- The meat sauce can be prepared in advance, making this a practical way to use a large batch of cooked sauce.
- The potatoes are baked as halves rather than being sliced or mashed.
- A subsequent variation using a paprika-based pork sauce also worked well.
- The recipe requires a substantial baking tray or multiple trays to accommodate the potatoes and filling.

## Testing and development

### Observations

- The dish was a success and is considered a favourite.
- The combination of 1.5 kg potatoes, approximately 1.6 kg meat sauce, 500 g black beans and 300 g cheese worked well.
- Pickled jalapeños provided a useful contrast to the rich filling.
- A later variation using paprika-based meat sauce also worked very well.

### Future improvements

- No specific changes to the tested recipe are required.
- Different prepared meat sauces can be used to create variations, but their seasoning and moisture content should be assessed individually.
