## Method

### Preparation

1. Cook the rice according to the packet instructions.
2. Cool the cooked rice promptly and refrigerate until needed. Cold rice is easier to stir-fry without becoming sticky.
3. Finely slice the pointed cabbage.
4. Peel and cut the carrots into small pieces or thin strips.
5. Finely chop the garlic and slice the spring onions.
6. Prepare the shelled edamame.
7. Beat the eggs in a separate bowl.

### Cooking

1. Heat a little neutral oil in a large frying pan or wok.
2. Scramble the eggs until just set. Remove them from the pan and set aside.
3. Add a little more oil and fry the carrots until they begin to soften.
4. Add the cabbage, garlic and edamame. Continue stir-frying until the vegetables are cooked but retain some texture.
5. Add the cooked rice. Stir-fry thoroughly, breaking up any clumps.
6. Add the light soy sauce and mix until evenly distributed.
7. Return the scrambled eggs to the pan.
8. Fold in the spring onions and sesame oil.
9. Taste and adjust the seasoning before serving.

For a batch of this size, cook in separate batches if the pan is too small. Overcrowding will cause the ingredients to steam rather than fry.

## Notes

The ingredient quantities reflect the successful enlarged batch prepared in October 2026. The quantities of spring onions and garlic come from the original recipe and were not separately confirmed during that preparation. The listed sauce is also the original documented version; the exact amount used during the successful batch was not independently confirmed.

Cool cooked rice promptly and refrigerate. Do not leave it standing at room temperature for several hours before serving.

## Testing and development

### Observations

- The dish was a success and is considered a favourite.
- The sauce was insufficient for the quantity of rice and vegetables.
- The soy flavour became less pronounced after several hours of storage.

### Future improvements

- Increase the sauce quantity and strengthen the soy flavour.
- Reserve some sauce to add when reheating.
- Test the proposed alternative sauce containing oyster sauce, or a vegetarian oyster-style alternative. The oyster sauce version would no longer be vegetarian.
