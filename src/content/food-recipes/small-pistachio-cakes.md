## Method

### Pistachio mixture

Process the pistachios and sugar in a food processor.

Add the egg whites and whisk in a saucepan for a couple of minutes.

### Butter mixture

Melt the butter.

Sift the powdered sugar and wheat flour into a bowl and whisk together with the pistachio mixture.

### Rest

Combine everything, transfer to a piping bag and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.

### Bake

Pipe the batter into silicone muffin moulds.

Bake at 180°C fan for 10-15 minutes, then at 150°C fan for another 5-10 minutes.
