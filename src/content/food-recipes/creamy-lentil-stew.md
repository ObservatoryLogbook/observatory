## Method

### Preparation

1. Peel and dice the carrots into small pieces.
2. Chop the onions and finely chop the garlic.
3. Rinse the green and red lentils separately.
4. Prepare the vegetable stock.

### Cooking

1. Heat a little oil in a large saucepan.
2. Add the onions and cook until softened.
3. Add the garlic, smoked paprika, sweet paprika, thyme and tomato purée. Stir and cook for approximately 1 minute.
4. Add the carrots, green lentils, chopped tomatoes and approximately 1.2 litres of vegetable stock.
5. Simmer gently for 15 minutes.
6. Add the red lentils and the remaining 300 ml stock.
7. Continue simmering for approximately 15–20 minutes, stirring regularly, until the lentils are tender. Add extra liquid if necessary.
8. Add the frozen spinach and cooking cream.
9. Simmer for another 5 minutes, ensuring the spinach is fully heated through.
10. Season with salt, black pepper and 1–2 tbsp lemon juice.

## Notes

- Green lentils provide texture, while red lentils soften and thicken the stew.
- The stew should remain slightly more fluid than the desired final consistency when cooking finishes, as the lentils continue absorbing liquid.
- Add extra stock when reheating if the stew has thickened.
- The ingredient quantities represent the exact recipe followed during the first test.
- The original seasoning is retained here for documentation. See the testing notes before preparing the dish again.

## Testing and development

### Observations

- The first preparation was successful, with a particularly good overall consistency and flavour.
- The stew could benefit from more pronounced seasoning.
- The 2 tsp smoked paprika produced an unpleasant smoky flavour for one taster, reminiscent of an ashtray.
- Smoked paprika should therefore be substantially reduced or omitted when preparing the dish again.

### Future improvements

- Test a version without smoked paprika.
- Increase the sweet paprika and other non-smoky seasonings to provide greater flavour intensity.
- Consider increasing the thyme or introducing a little ground cumin, but test the changes before incorporating them into the permanent recipe.
- Preserve the existing lentil, vegetable and liquid proportions, which worked well in the first test.
