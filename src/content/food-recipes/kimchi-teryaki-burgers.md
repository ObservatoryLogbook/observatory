## Method

### Make the teriyaki sauce

Bring the soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, cornstarch and honey to a boil.

### Make the kimchi mayo

Chop the pointed cabbage and blend with the kimchi and crème fraîche.

### Make the burgers

Mix the ginger, garlic, minced beef, soy sauce, sesame oil and pepper.

Shape into 4 patties.

Fry the patties in a pan.

Add the teriyaki sauce and glaze the patties.

Sprinkle with sesame seeds.

### Serve

Serve the burgers with the kimchi mayo.
