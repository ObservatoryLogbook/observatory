## Method

Preheat the oven to 180°C.

Melt the dark chocolate and coconut oil together.

Whisk the egg whites (or eggs) and brown sugar in a mixing bowl. Stir in the remaining brownie ingredients.

Spread the brownie mixture in a roughly 20 × 20 cm baking tin.

Whisk all the cheesecake ingredients together until smooth.

Spoon dollops of the cheesecake mixture over the brownie batter and swirl with a knife.

Bake for 35–40 minutes.
