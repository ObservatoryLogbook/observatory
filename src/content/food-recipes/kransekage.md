## Method

Mix one third of the egg whites with the icing sugar.

Add the marzipan in smaller pieces and mix until combined.

Gradually mix in the remaining egg whites.

Pipe onto a baking tray and leave uncovered for approximately 30 minutes.

Bake at 200°C for 8–10 minutes.
