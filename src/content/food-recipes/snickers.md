## Method

### Prepare the base

Blend the dates, peanuts and peanut butter with a little water.

Transfer to a square tin and press firmly into an even layer.

Refrigerate until firm.

### Coat

Temper the dark chocolate.

Coat the chilled base with the tempered chocolate.
