## Method

### Poolish

Mix the bread flour, water and yeast. Leave overnight.

### Prepare the salt mixture

Mix the water and salt and set aside.

### Mix the dough

Mix the water, yeast, bread flour and poolish.

Autolyse for 30 minutes, then mix with the salt mixture.

### Bulk fermentation

Bulk ferment for 3 1/2 hours, turning every hour.

### Divide and shape

Divide into 350 g pieces.

Rest for 20 minutes.

Shape into baguettes.

### Final proof

Proof for 1-1 1/2 hours.

### Bake

Bake with steam at 245°C for 25 minutes.

After 20 minutes, open the oven to let out the steam.
