## Method

Blend all the cake ingredients until smooth.

Press the mixture into a greased 20 cm round cake tin and bake at 170°C for 40–50 minutes.

For the cinnamon fudge, soak the dates in boiling water for 5 minutes.

Blend the dates with the cinnamon and salt. Reserve the soaking water and add a little at a time while blending until the desired consistency is reached.

Add the melted plant-based butter a little at a time and blend until smooth.

Spread the cinnamon fudge over the cooled cake and decorate with blueberries.
