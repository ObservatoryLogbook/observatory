## Method

Mix wet ingredients first, then add dry ingredients and combine. 

Set on a baking tray with a quarter-cup ice-cream scoop. 

### Bake

Bake at 175°C for 17-18 minutes.

For crispier cookies, bake for 20-22 minutes.
