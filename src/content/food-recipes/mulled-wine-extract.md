## Method

### Make the spiced syrup

Combine the vanilla pod, sugar, muscovado sugar, nutmeg, cloves, cinnamon sticks, cardamom pods, peppercorns, allspice, star anise, ginger and orange juice.

Boil together for 5 minutes.

### Infuse

Add the lemon and orange slices, schnapps, dark rum and port.

Pour into sterilised preserving jars.

Leave the mixture to infuse for 1-3 weeks.

### Strain and store

Strain the extract, first through a sieve and then through a cloth.

Store in sterilised jars.

### Use

Use 2 dl red wine with 1/2 tbsp extract.
