## Method

### Make the dough

Whisk the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.

Whisk in the egg yolks one at a time.

Add the flour, salt and baking powder.

### Make the macaroon filling

Whisk the egg whites until stiff.

Mix the icing sugar, crushed *tvebakker* and almond essence into the egg whites.

### Assemble

Line a small roasting tin, approximately 25 × 20 cm, with the dough. Press the dough well into the corners.

Pour the filling over the base.

### Bake

Bake at 200°C for 30 minutes.
