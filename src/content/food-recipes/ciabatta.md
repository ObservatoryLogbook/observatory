## Method

### Poolish

Mix the water, flour and fresh yeast. Leave overnight in the fridge.

### Dough

Combine the flour, water, salt and poolish.

Mix in a stand mixer for 5 minutes.

Leave to rise for 2 hours adn 15 minutes, turning the dough every 45 minutes.

### Shape and proof

Shape the dough.

Leave to proof in couches for 20 minutes.

### Bake

Bake at 230°C with steam for 18 minutes.
