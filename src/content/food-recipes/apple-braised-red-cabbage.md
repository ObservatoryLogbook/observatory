## Method

### Preparation

1. Remove any damaged outer leaves from the red cabbage.
2. Remove the hard core and finely shred the cabbage.
3. Thinly slice the apples and red onion.
4. Measure out the apple juice and vinegar.

### Cooking

1. Heat the butter or oil in a large saucepan.
2. Add the red onion and cook gently until softened.
3. Add the shredded red cabbage and sliced apples.
4. Pour in the apple juice and apple cider vinegar.
5. Add 1 tbsp brown sugar or syrup, together with salt and black pepper.
6. Stir thoroughly and cover with a lid.
7. Simmer gently for approximately 45–60 minutes, stirring occasionally.
8. Add a little water if the cabbage begins to dry out or catch on the bottom.
9. Continue cooking until the cabbage is soft and tender.
10. Taste and adjust the balance of sweetness, acidity and salt. Add the remaining sugar or syrup only if needed.

## Notes

- The cabbage should be soft and moist rather than crunchy.
- The final balance should combine the natural sweetness of apples with a noticeable but pleasant acidity.
- The amount of additional sugar or syrup depends on the sweetness of the apples and apple juice.
- The cabbage can be prepared ahead of time and reheated.
- Serve as a side dish with [Nordic Pulled Pork with Apple and Thyme](./nordic-pulled-pork.md).
