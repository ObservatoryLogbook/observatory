## Method

Soak the gelatine sheets in cold water for about 10 minutes. Strain them, and gently melt them over low heat. 

Mix together eggs and sugar until light. 

Add in lemon zest and lemon juice and the melted gelatine. 

Whip the heavy cream. Gently fold in the egg mixture.
