## Method

### Preparation

1. Cut the chicken breasts into bite-sized pieces.
2. Clean the leeks thoroughly and slice into rings.
3. Slice the mushrooms.
4. Finely chop the onions and garlic.
5. Prepare the chicken stock.

### Cooking

1. Heat a little oil in a large frying pan or saucepan.
2. Brown the chicken pieces in batches over medium-high heat. Transfer to a plate.
3. Add the mushrooms to the same pan and fry until they have released their moisture and begun to brown. Cook in batches if necessary.
4. Add the onions and leeks, with a little additional oil if needed.
5. Cook for approximately 5–8 minutes, until softened.
6. Add the garlic and thyme, and cook for another minute.
7. Return the chicken to the pan.
8. Add the chicken stock, cooking cream and Dijon mustard.
9. Stir thoroughly and bring to a gentle simmer.
10. Simmer for approximately 10–15 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked and the sauce has thickened slightly.
11. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
12. Meanwhile, cook the rice separately according to the packet instructions.
13. Serve the creamy chicken, leek and mushroom sauce over the rice.

## Notes

- The mushrooms should be browned rather than steamed. Frying them in batches helps develop a deeper flavour.
- The leeks should soften without becoming heavily browned.
- The sauce is deliberately a little more fluid than in the original pasta version, as it is intended to accompany rice.
- Cook the rice separately to maintain control over its texture and the consistency of the sauce.
- The dish can be prepared ahead of time. Refrigerate cooked rice promptly and reheat thoroughly, avoiding repeated cooling and reheating.
- The original version used 200 ml chicken stock and 300 g dry protein pasta. It was successfully tested.
- This version uses 300 ml stock and 350 g dry rice.

## Testing and development

### Observations

- The original chicken, leek and mushroom sauce was successful when served with protein pasta.
- The rice variation and increased stock quantity have not yet been tested.

### Future improvements

- Evaluate whether 300 ml chicken stock produces the right sauce consistency for serving with rice.
- Assess whether the rice provides a satisfactory balance with the creamy sauce.
- Adjust the amount of thyme, Dijon mustard or black pepper if the flavour needs more depth.
- Record any changes after the first preparation.
