## Method

### Make the dough

Whisk the butter, brown sugar, dark syrup and vanilla sugar until light and fluffy.

Add the egg.

Mix the flour, ground ginger and baking powder, then fold into the mixture.

### Chill and shape

Chill the dough in the refrigerator for 20-30 minutes.

Divide into 4 portions.

Roll each portion into logs and divide each log into 20 pieces.

### Bake

Bake at 175°C fan for 10-12 minutes.

### Make the lemon cream

Whisk the quark, icing sugar, lemon zest and vanilla carefully until combined.
