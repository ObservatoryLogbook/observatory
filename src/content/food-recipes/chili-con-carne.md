## Method

### Chili oil

Heat the oil with the sliced chili and bay leaves over low heat for 10 minutes. Strain the oil and reserve it.

### Beef

Cut the beef into approximately 2 × 5 cm pieces. Brown in three batches in the chili oil. Remove from the pot and set aside.

### Aromatics

Dice the red onions and finely chop the garlic and chili. Fry in the pot, then remove and set aside.

### Spices and braise

Toast the dried spices briefly in the pot. Return the beef and onion mixture, then add the diced carrots and tomato paste. Mix well.

Add the bourbon and allow the alcohol to evaporate. Add the strong coffee and chopped tomatoes. Simmer for 2½–3 hours.

### Finish

Add the diced bell peppers and beans and simmer for another 30 minutes.

Finish with the dark chocolate and brown sugar and adjust the seasoning to taste.

### To serve

Serve with crème fraîche and good bread, or rice. If serving without bread or rice, increase the amount of beans.
