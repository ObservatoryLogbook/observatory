## Method

### Preparation

1. Defrost the reserved pulled pork in the refrigerator if previously frozen.
2. Peel the potatoes and cut into evenly sized chunks.
3. Divide the cauliflower into florets.
4. Finely chop the onions and dice the carrots.
5. Grate the Cheddar.
6. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan.

### Potato and cauliflower mash

1. Boil the potatoes in lightly salted water until completely tender.
2. Steam or boil the cauliflower separately until tender.
3. Drain both vegetables thoroughly.
4. Allow the cauliflower to release as much excess moisture as possible. This is important for preventing a watery mash.
5. Mash the potatoes and cauliflower together.
6. Add the butter and gradually incorporate 100–150 ml milk until the mash is smooth but relatively firm.
7. Season with salt, black pepper and a little nutmeg.

### Pulled pork filling

1. Heat a little oil in a large frying pan or saucepan.
2. Add the onions and carrots.
3. Fry gently until softened.
4. Add the prepared pulled pork and frozen peas.
5. Pour in approximately 200 ml reserved cooking liquid.
6. Stir and simmer gently until the filling is heated through.
7. Add additional cooking liquid if necessary. The filling should be moist and cohesive, but not excessively watery.
8. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.

### Assembly and baking

1. Transfer the pulled pork filling to a large ovenproof dish.
2. Spread the potato and cauliflower mash evenly over the filling.
3. Use a fork to create a lightly textured surface.
4. Sprinkle the grated Cheddar evenly over the top.
5. Bake at 200°C fan for approximately 20–25 minutes, until the cheese is golden and the filling is piping hot.
6. Allow the pie to stand for 5–10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- The pulled pork is prepared separately using [Nordic Pulled Pork with Apple and Thyme](./nordic-pulled-pork.md).
- Reserve both shredded pork and cooking liquid from the original preparation.
- The mash uses equal weights of potatoes and cauliflower.
- Drain the cauliflower thoroughly to prevent the topping from becoming watery.
- The filling should contain sufficient sauce to remain moist during baking.
- If assembled in advance and refrigerated, allow approximately 35–45 minutes for baking, depending on the depth of the dish. Check that the centre is thoroughly heated.
- The recipe is designed to use previously prepared pulled pork, reducing active preparation time on the day of serving.
