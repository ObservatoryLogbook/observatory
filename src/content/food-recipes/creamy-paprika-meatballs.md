## Method

### Preparation

1. Finely grate the onion for the meatballs.
2. Mix the minced meat with salt, then add the onion, eggs, oats, milk and black pepper.
3. Stir until evenly combined and leave the mixture to rest for approximately 15 minutes.
4. Finely chop the remaining onions and garlic.
5. Dice the red pepper very finely.
6. Peel and chop the potatoes, and divide the cauliflower into florets.

### Cauliflower and potato mash

1. Boil the potatoes in lightly salted water until tender.
2. Steam or boil the cauliflower separately until completely tender.
3. Drain both thoroughly, allowing excess moisture to evaporate.
4. Mash the potatoes and cauliflower together.
5. Add the butter and enough milk to achieve a smooth, soft consistency.
6. Season with salt, black pepper and a little nutmeg if desired.
7. Keep warm until serving.

### Creamy paprika sauce

1. Melt the butter in a large, wide saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the finely chopped onions and red pepper.
3. Cook gently for 8–10 minutes, until very soft but not browned.
4. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
5. Stir in the sweet paprika and cook for approximately 20–30 seconds, taking care not to burn it.
6. Sprinkle over the flour and stir thoroughly.
7. Gradually add the stock, stirring continuously to prevent lumps.
8. Add the cooking cream and bring to a gentle simmer.
9. Cook for approximately 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
10. Season with salt and black pepper.

### Cooking the meatballs

1. Using two spoons or damp hands, shape the meat mixture into medium-sized meatballs.
2. Carefully lower the meatballs into the gently simmering paprika sauce, working in batches if necessary.
3. Cover and simmer gently for approximately 15–20 minutes, depending on their size.
4. Avoid vigorous boiling or stirring until the meatballs have firmed up.
5. Check that the meatballs are cooked through.
6. Taste the sauce and adjust the seasoning.

### Serving

Serve the meatballs with generous amounts of creamy paprika sauce over the cauliflower and potato mash.

## Notes

- The intended sauce is smooth, creamy and pale orange-pink, without tomato.
- Sweet paprika provides colour and flavour without the smokiness of smoked paprika.
- The onion and red pepper should be chopped very finely so they soften into the sauce.
- The meatballs are poached directly in the sauce, similarly to Danish meatballs in curry sauce.
- The cauliflower and potato mash is based on a previously successful combination.
- Drain the cauliflower particularly well to avoid a watery mash.
- The dish can be prepared ahead of time and gently reheated. Add a little milk or stock if the sauce becomes too thick.
