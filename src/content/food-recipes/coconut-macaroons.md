## Method

### Make the mixture

Mix the melted butter, eggs, sugar, desiccated coconut and lime zest together.

### Shape

Shape into 15 peaks, using approximately 1 tablespoon of mixture for each.

### Bake

Bake at 175°C for approximately 17 minutes.

### Finish

Dip either the top or the bottom of each macaroon in melted chocolate.
