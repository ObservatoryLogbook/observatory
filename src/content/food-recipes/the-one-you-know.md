## Method

Mix the sugar, wheat flour, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt.

Whisk together the melted butter, milk, eggs and strong coffee.

Combine the wet and dry ingredients.

Pour the batter into a small roasting tin and bake at 175°C for approximately 35 minutes.

For the topping, combine the butter, icing sugar, desiccated coconut, cocoa powder and strong coffee in a saucepan. Heat gently until combined.

Spread the topping over the cake while it is still slightly warm.
