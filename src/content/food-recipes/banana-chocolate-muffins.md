## Method

### Make the batter

Whisk the eggs and sugar until light and fluffy.

Mix the melted butter, flour, baking powder, salt and vanilla sugar into the egg mixture.

Fold in the mashed bananas.

Fold in two thirds of the chopped dark chocolate.

### Fill the muffin tins

Divide the batter between 12 muffin cups, about 1 ice cream scoop per cup.

Sprinkle with the remaining chopped chocolate.

### Bake

Bake at 200°C for approximately 18 minutes.
