## Method

### Cooking the rice pudding

1. Pour approximately 200 ml of the milk into a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan.
2. Add the pudding rice and bring gently to a simmer, stirring frequently.
3. Gradually add the remaining milk.
4. Bring the mixture back to a gentle simmer, stirring thoroughly to prevent the rice from sticking to the bottom.
5. Reduce the heat to low and cook for approximately 35–45 minutes, stirring regularly.
6. Continue until the rice is tender and the pudding is creamy.
7. Add a little additional milk if the mixture becomes too thick before the rice is fully cooked.
8. Stir in the salt.

### Adding the protein

1. Blend the cottage cheese until completely smooth.
2. In a separate bowl, mix the vanilla protein powder with a little cold milk to form a smooth, pourable mixture.
3. Remove the cooked rice pudding from the heat and allow it to cool slightly for approximately 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the blended cottage cheese.
5. Gradually incorporate the protein powder mixture, stirring continuously.
6. Add additional milk if needed to achieve a soft, creamy consistency.
7. Taste and add liquid sweetener if desired.
8. Warm gently if necessary, without bringing the pudding back to a boil.

### Serving

Serve warm with cinnamon, optionally mixed with sweetener, and a small amount of butter.

## Notes

- The cottage cheese should be blended thoroughly to avoid noticeable curds.
- Add the protein powder only after removing the saucepan from the heat.
- The pudding may thicken considerably as it cools, particularly because of the added protein.
- Keep additional skimmed milk available for adjusting the consistency.
- Vanilla protein powder contributes sweetness, so taste before adding extra sweetener.
- The recipe is designed as a protein-enriched version of traditional Danish rice pudding.
- Refrigerate leftovers promptly and reheat thoroughly, adding extra milk as necessary. Avoid repeated cooling and reheating.
