## Method

### Prepare

Mix the cream cheese and crème fraîche. Whip the cream and fold it into the cheese mixture. 

Can be flavoured with vanilla sugar and powdered sugar.

Crush the Bastogne biscuits and mix with melted butter. 

### Assemble

Layer everything together. Top with green grapes.
