## Method

### Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 210°C fan.
2. Cut the cauliflower and broccoli into medium-sized florets.
3. Thinly slice the red onion.
4. Drain and rinse the white beans.
5. Remove the seeds from the pomegranates, discarding the bitter white membranes.
6. Roughly chop the almonds and parsley.
7. Crumble the feta into bite-sized pieces.

### Roasting the vegetables

1. Divide the cauliflower between large baking trays, leaving enough space for the florets to roast rather than steam.
2. Toss with some of the olive oil, cumin, paprika, salt and black pepper.
3. Roast for approximately 30–35 minutes, turning halfway through, until tender with well-browned edges.
4. Toss the broccoli with the remaining olive oil and seasoning.
5. Roast the broccoli on separate trays for approximately 20–25 minutes, until tender and lightly browned.
6. Add the sliced red onion to the roasting vegetables during the final 15–20 minutes, allowing it to soften and develop some colour.

### Lemon and garlic skyr dressing

1. Combine the skyr, lemon juice, crushed garlic and olive oil in a bowl.
2. Season with salt and black pepper.
3. Add cold water a little at a time until the dressing is creamy but pourable.
4. Taste and adjust the acidity and seasoning.

### Assembly

1. Transfer the warm roasted cauliflower, broccoli and red onion to a very large serving bowl or platter.
2. Add the drained white beans and toss gently with the warm vegetables. The residual heat should warm the beans.
3. Scatter the crumbled feta over the salad.
4. Add the pomegranate seeds, chopped almonds and fresh parsley.
5. Serve with the lemon and garlic skyr dressing, either drizzled over the salad or on the side.

## Notes

- The cauliflower and broccoli have different roasting times and are best cooked separately.
- Use several baking trays if necessary. Overcrowding will cause the vegetables to steam rather than develop browned edges.
- The white beans should be warmed by the roasted vegetables without being cooked until they break down.
- Add the feta, pomegranate seeds, almonds and parsley immediately before serving.
- Keep the skyr dressing separate until serving to preserve the contrast between the warm vegetables and cool dressing.
- Sweet paprika can replace smoked paprika in the same quantity. This substitution has not yet been tested in this recipe.
- The quantities produce a very large salad.

### Future improvements

- Evaluate the balance between the earthy roasted vegetables, salty feta and sweet-tart pomegranate seeds.
- Assess whether 2 tsp sweet paprika provides enough flavour alongside the cumin, without introducing an unwanted smoky note.
- Check whether the almonds provide sufficient crunch or would benefit from being lightly toasted.
- Evaluate whether the white beans warm through sufficiently during assembly.
- Assess the dressing quantity and consistency, particularly whether more lemon juice or salt is needed.
- Record the final roasting times and any adjustments to the vegetable quantities.
