## Method

Sauté the Hokkaido pumpkin, onion, garlic, carrots, potatoes, chilli, cumin and ginger in the olive oil.

Add the vegetable stock and simmer for 15–18 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.

Blend until smooth.

Stir in the whipping cream and season to taste with lemon juice, salt and pepper.

Serve with roasted pumpkin seeds and your choice of Greek yoghurt, bacon and apple.
