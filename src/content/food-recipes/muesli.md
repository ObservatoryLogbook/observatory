## Method

Mix all the dry ingredients.

Melt the honey or syrup together with the oil, cinnamon and salt. Add to the dry ingredients and mix thoroughly.

Bake at 160°C fan for 20–25 minutes.

Optionally, add 30–40 g protein powder to the dry ingredients before baking.
