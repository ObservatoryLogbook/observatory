## Method

Blend everything except the juice from 1 orange, the apple and the cranberries.

Fold the apple and cranberries into the batter.

Marinate another thinly sliced carrot separately in the juice from the remaining orange, then add it to the batter.

Transfer the batter to a greased 20 cm cake tin and bake at 175°C for 40–50 minutes.
