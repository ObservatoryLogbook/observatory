## Method

### Prepare the spinach sauce

Mix the spinach, Mornay sauce, water, lemon zest and juice, salt and pepper.

### Assemble

Layer the spinach sauce, salmon and lasagne sheets.

### Bake

Bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.

Add the grated cheese and bake for another 20 minutes.

### Rest

Leave to rest for 10 minutes before serving.
