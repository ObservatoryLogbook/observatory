## Method

### Make the rough puff pastry

Mix the lukewarm water and yeast, add sugar, margarine or butter and eggs. Mix briefly and leave to rest for 20 minutes.

Add the flour and salt, and mix the dough together. Do not knead the dough. Let it rest for 30 minutes. 

Roll out the dough, fold it, roll it out again and fold again. Then roll it out. 

### Make the filling

Melt the margarine, sugar, cardamom and cinnamon. 

### Assemble

Divide the dough into 4 pieces and shape into kringles.

Add the filling, raisins and hazelnuts.

Leave to rise for 15 minutes.

Brush with egg and sprinkle with pearl sugar and hazelnuts.

### Bake

Bake at 220°C for 14 minutes.
