## Method

### Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan.
2. Peel the celeriac and carrots. Cut into bite-sized pieces.
3. Peel the red onions and cut into wedges.
4. Clean the mushrooms and halve or quarter any large ones.
5. Rinse the beluga lentils thoroughly.
6. Roughly chop the walnuts and parsley.

### Cooking

1. Arrange the celeriac, carrots and red onions on baking trays. Toss with olive oil, salt and black pepper.
2. Roast until tender and lightly caramelised, approximately 30–40 minutes depending on the size of the pieces.
3. Roast the mushrooms separately, or add them to a tray later in the cooking process. Cook until tender and lightly browned.
4. Meanwhile, cook the beluga lentils in plenty of water according to the packet instructions, typically 20–30 minutes. They should be tender but retain their shape.
5. Drain the lentils thoroughly.
6. Whisk together 3 tbsp olive oil, 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar and 1 tsp Dijon mustard. Season with salt and black pepper.
7. Toss the warm lentils with the dressing.
8. Fold in the roasted vegetables and mushrooms.
9. Taste and adjust the seasoning.

### Whipped feta

1. Combine the feta and skyr in a bowl or food processor.
2. Add lemon juice, garlic and black pepper.
3. Blend or stir until smooth and creamy.
4. Taste and adjust the lemon and seasoning. The feta will already contribute salt.

### Serving

1. Transfer the warm lentil and vegetable mixture to a large serving dish.
2. Scatter the walnuts and chopped parsley over the salad.
3. Serve with generous spoonfuls of whipped feta, either on top or alongside.

## Notes

- The lentils should retain some bite rather than becoming soft or mushy.
- Dressing the lentils while warm helps distribute the flavour.
- Roast the vegetables in a sufficiently thin layer to encourage caramelisation rather than steaming.
- The mushrooms can release considerable moisture, so roasting them separately may give a better result.
- The whipped feta is an essential component of the dish rather than an optional garnish.
