## Method

Heat the olive oil in a pan. Add the onion, mushrooms, squash, carrots and garlic and sauté until well browned.

Add the sun-dried tomatoes, chopped tomatoes, basil, thyme, salt, pepper and rinsed beluga lentils. Cover and cook for 5 minutes.

Layer the lentil sauce with the lasagne sheets and mornay sauce. Finish with the grated cheese.

Bake at 225°C for 25–35 minutes.

Leave to rest for 10 minutes before serving.
