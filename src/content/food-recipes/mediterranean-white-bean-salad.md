## Method

### Preparation

1. Drain and rinse the white beans thoroughly. Allow any excess water to drain away.
2. Dice the cucumbers into bite-sized pieces.
3. Cut the tomatoes into irregular, bite-sized chunks.
4. Finely slice the red onion.
5. Dice the red pepper.
6. Roughly chop the parsley.
7. Drain the olives if necessary.
8. Crumble the feta into relatively large pieces.

### Dressing

1. Combine 3 tbsp olive oil, 3 tbsp lemon juice and 1 tsp dried oregano.
2. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
3. Whisk until combined.

### Assembly

1. Combine the white beans, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, red pepper and olives in a large serving bowl.
2. Add the chopped parsley.
3. Pour over the dressing and toss gently to distribute it evenly.
4. Scatter the crumbled feta over the salad.
5. Add generous spoonfuls of cottage cheese.
6. Serve immediately, optionally with bread.

## Notes

- Use 750 g drained white beans, equivalent to approximately three standard tins, depending on the brand.
- The combination of feta and cottage cheese provides both saltiness and creaminess.
- Add the cottage cheese towards the end rather than mixing it thoroughly into the salad.
- The salad is intended to be fresh and juicy, with a substantial proportion of vegetables.
- Bread is optional rather than an essential component.

## Testing and development

### Observations

- The salad was successful, with a fresh, well-balanced flavour.
- The combination of white beans, feta and cottage cheese worked particularly well.
- The generous quantity of vegetables made the dish substantial without feeling heavy.
- The original proportions produced a satisfying complete meal.
