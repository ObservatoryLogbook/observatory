## Method

### Prepare the sauce

Mix the tomato purée, coconut milk, Greek yoghurt and heavy cream first.

Add the onion, garlic, ginger, spices, salt and butter and mix.

### Slow cook

Put everything in a slow cooker.

Cook on low for at least 6 hours.

### Serve

Serve with brown basmati rice and vegetables, such as cauliflower and broccoli.
