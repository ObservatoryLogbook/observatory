## Method

### Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 210°C fan.
2. Wash the sweet potatoes and cut them in half lengthways.
3. Brush the cut surfaces lightly with oil.
4. Finely chop the onions and garlic.
5. Dice the red pepper.
6. Drain and rinse the black beans.

### Cooking

1. Place the sweet potato halves on baking trays.
2. Roast for approximately 35–45 minutes, until tender. Adjust the cooking time according to their size.
3. Meanwhile, heat a little oil in a large frying pan or saucepan.
4. Brown the minced beef, breaking it into smaller pieces.
5. Add the onions and cook until softened.
6. Add the garlic, red pepper, tomato purée, cumin, smoked paprika and oregano. Stir thoroughly.
7. Add the black beans, sweetcorn and approximately 100 ml water or stock.
8. Simmer for 10–15 minutes, adding more liquid if necessary to prevent the filling from becoming dry.
9. Season with salt and black pepper.
10. Remove the tender sweet potatoes from the oven.
11. Spoon the beef and bean filling over the potato halves.
12. Sprinkle with grated cheese.
13. Return to the oven until the cheese has melted and the filling is hot.
14. Serve with natural skyr and pickled jalapeños.

## Notes

- The ingredient quantities are taken from the original planned recipe.
- The exact quantities used during the subsequent preparation were not independently recorded.
- The filling should remain moist rather than becoming dry during simmering.
- The skyr and jalapeños provide a cool, tangy contrast to the rich filling.
- This is a separate variation of [Mexican Loaded Potatoes](/engineering/food/mexican-loaded-potatoes/), which uses ordinary baking potatoes and a prepared meat sauce.

## Testing and development

### Observations

- The dish worked well as a variation of Mexican Loaded Potatoes.
- The sweetness of the sweet potatoes was less appealing than the flavour of ordinary potatoes.
- The original version with ordinary potatoes remains the preferred option.

### Future improvements

- Use ordinary baking potatoes instead of sweet potatoes for a less sweet result.
- No other specific changes have been identified.
