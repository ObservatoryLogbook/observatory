## Method

Blend all the cake ingredients until smooth.

Press the mixture into a greased 20 cm round cake tin.

Bake at 175°C for 40–50 minutes.

For the glaze, blend all the ingredients, adjusting the amount of lemon to taste and desired consistency.

Spread the glaze over the cake once it has cooled for about 30 minutes.
