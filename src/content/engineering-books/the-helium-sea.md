Very interesting ideas and concepts, but much harder to follow than previous Peter F. Hamilton books. There were too many different characters who are all important, and even though I had only read a relatively small book between the first book in the series and this one, I really had to spend some time catching up. For this, reading the book on Kindle doesn't help:had it been a physical book, I could easily have flipped to the first few pages for reminding myself of the characters.
