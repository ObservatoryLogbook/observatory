First book in the Exodus two-book series. There is a surprisingly steep learning curve for getting into the story, considering it is a Peter F. Hamilton book. He tends to serve the science fiction elements more straightforward. At the same time, the different racces of humanity and their naming conventions makes it surprisingly difficult to read. 

However, once you are past the first quarter of the book, it all starts to make sense and it comes together very nicely.
